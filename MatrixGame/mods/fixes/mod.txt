# CaptureNoTimeout: the 10 s limit of a robot with a base (for a new robot stuck on its lift) no longer kills
# a robot whose capture is held up (the original killed it and closed the base).
# StopInPlace: Stop halts each robot where it stands (the original sent the group on to free places round
# its centre).
# The other battle fixes of PBEngine's list are already in this engine's source, mod or not: the bomb's blast from
# the robot's centre (CMatrixRobotAI::BigBoom: GetGeoCenter), the Firewall's BOMB_PROTECT on WEAPON_BIGBOOM
# (CMatrixRobot::Damage), a mortar shell exploding at its path's end (CTrajectory::CalcPoint clamps, MO_Bomb_Takt),
# the flamethrower's reach from its range with the Locator (ModifyDist before CreateFlame), the radar round the
# driven robot (DrawRadar).
